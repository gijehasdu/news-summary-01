World news: Wednesday 11 November 2026

1. Abu Dhabi (Reuters): Russia–Ukraine talks resumed for the first time in nine months, agreeing on a large prisoner release but deadlocking over territory. Kyiv refused to give up all Donbas; US envoy Jared Kushner said “no trust.”
2. Antalya, Türkiye (AFP): COP31’s leaders’ session opened with 102 heads of state and 300 ministers; Türkiye hosted for the first time. UN chief António Guterres said slow climate response put the world “on a path to destruction.”
3. Strait of Hormuz (Reuters): Iran said it stays shut until Washington meets seven conditions for talks. Three tankers caught fire Tuesday after hitting mines; 37 shipping attacks near the strait were counted this month.
4. Washington (BBC): Prosecutors charged three Iran-backed operatives over about 20 US and European plots, including one targeting RAF Fairford, England. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent moved onto a military base amid Iranian threats.
5. DR Congo (Reuters): Tuesday: 11,042 confirmed Ebola cases, at least 5,387 deaths; the second-largest and second-deadliest outbreak on record.
6. Jerusalem (Reuters): A new Knesset was sworn in without a government after the deadlocked 27 October election. Gadi Eisenkot has until 6 December to form one; Netanyahu is caretaker PM.
7. Mississippi Senate race (AP): Democrat Scott Colom led incumbent Cindy Hyde-Smith by over 3,000 votes, with 98% counted. Uncalled; certification due Friday. No Democrat has won a Mississippi Senate race since 1982.
8. Florida (Reuters): A judge refused to dismiss Trump’s $10bn defamation suit against the BBC over its Panorama edit of his 6 January 2021 speech. BBC must answer by 1 December.
9. Markets (AP): US October inflation rose 0.4% Tuesday versus 0.3% forecast; a December Fed hike is near certain. Asian shares rebounded: Nikkei +1.3%, Kospi about +2.5%.
10. Washington (BBC): The House voted 211–204 to send the Hegseth impeachment resolution to committee.
