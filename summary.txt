World news — Wednesday, 11 November 2026

1. Abu Dhabi (Reuters): Russia and Ukraine held their first talks in nine months and agreed on a large prisoner release, but remained deadlocked over territory. Kyiv refused all of Donbas; US envoy Jared Kushner said there was “no trust”.
2. Antalya, Türkiye (AFP): COP31’s leaders’ session opened with 102 heads of state and 300 ministers, Türkiye’s first time hosting. UN chief António Guterres said slow climate action put the world “on a path to destruction”.
3. Strait of Hormuz (Reuters): Iran said the strait would stay shut until Washington met its seven conditions for talks. Three tankers caught fire Tuesday after hitting mines; 37 attacks on nearby shipping have been counted this month.
4. Washington (BBC): Prosecutors charged three Iran-backed operatives over about 20 plots in the US and Europe, including one targeting RAF Fairford in England. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent moved onto a military base amid Iranian threats.
5. DR Congo (Reuters): Tuesday’s figures showed 11,042 confirmed Ebola cases and at least 5,387 deaths, the second-largest and second-deadliest outbreak on record.
6. Jerusalem (Reuters): The new Knesset was sworn in without a government after the deadlocked 27 October election. Gadi Eisenkot has until 6 December to form one; Netanyahu remains caretaker prime minister.
7. Mississippi (AP): Democrat Scott Colom led incumbent Cindy Hyde-Smith by more than 3,000 votes with 98% counted; the race was uncalled. Certification is due Friday. No Democrat has won a Mississippi Senate race since 1982.
8. Florida (Reuters): A judge refused to dismiss Trump’s $10bn defamation suit against the BBC over a Panorama edit of his 6 January 2021 speech. The broadcaster must answer by 1 December.
9. Markets (AP): US October inflation rose 0.4%, above the 0.3% forecast; a December Fed rate hike was priced as near certain. Asian shares rebounded: Nikkei +1.3%, Kospi about +2.5%.
